Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NEX ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by Catini Martial Arts Academy, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

By using NEX you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, password (hashed).
  • Profile data: profile photo, belt rank, training history.
  • Waiver acceptance: timestamp, IP address, signature image.
  • Athlete assessment: weight, height, body measurements, training notes (when voluntarily provided by you to track your training progress).
  • Payment data: handled exclusively by our payment processor Stripe. We do not store full credit-card numbers on our servers.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Attendance records: time and date of check-ins at the academy via in-app QR-code scan.
  • Device information: operating system version, device model, app version. Used solely for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Authentication tokens: stored securely on-device to keep you logged in.

1.3 Health and fitness data (optional)

If you choose to connect a wearable device (such as Apple Watch or a Polar heart-rate monitor), NEX may read the following data from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android):

  • Workout sessions (type, start time, duration)
  • Active calories burned
  • Heart-rate samples (average and maximum)

We only read this data; we never write to your health record. Data is sent securely to our backend to display your training history inside the App. You can revoke this access at any time from your phone's Settings.

1.4 Camera and Photos

  • The camera is used only to scan the academy's QR code for check-in.
  • You may grant access to your photo library when uploading a profile picture.
  • We never access photos or camera in the background.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your student account.
  • To record class attendance and training progress.
  • To process membership payments and issue invoices.
  • To send notifications about classes, billing, and academy events.
  • To issue digital certificates (belt promotions, seminars).
  • To communicate with you about the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the App:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. (privacy policy)
  • Stripe — payment processing. (privacy policy)
  • Render — backend server hosting. (privacy policy)
  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — local-only health-data bridges; we read data on-device, the OS frameworks do not transmit anything to Apple or Google's servers on our behalf.
  • Expo / EAS — push notifications and over-the-air updates. (privacy policy)

4. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or accounting purposes (e.g. tax records of payments).

5. Your Rights

You may, at any time:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for health-data sharing (via phone Settings).
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at kevembusiness@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and Row-Level Security on our database. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If a minor (13–17) uses the App for training at the academy, registration must be performed by a parent or legal guardian who accepts this Policy on their behalf.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page. Material changes will be communicated within the App.
