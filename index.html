Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NEX ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by Nex Academy LLC, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

By using NEX you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, password (hashed).
  • Profile data: profile photo, belt rank, training history.
  • Waiver acceptance: timestamp, IP address, signature image.
  • Athlete assessment: weight, height, body measurements, training notes (when voluntarily provided by you to track your training progress).
  • Payment data: handled exclusively by our payment processor Stripe. We do not store full credit-card numbers on our servers.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Attendance records: time and date of check-ins at the academy via in-app QR-code scan.
  • Device information: operating system version, device model, app version. Used solely for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Authentication tokens: stored securely on-device to keep you logged in.

1.3 Health and fitness data (optional)

Connecting a fitness service is optional and nothing below is read until you tap Connect on the App's Fitness screen and grant permission on the screen your phone shows you. If you never connect one, NEX reads none of it. When you do connect, NEX may read the following data:

  • Apple HealthKit (iOS) — with your permission NEX requests read access to workout sessions (type, start, duration), active energy burned, heart-rate samples, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, walking, running and cycling distance, weight, body-fat percentage, lean body mass, sleep analysis, step count and exercise time. Of these, only workouts (type, start, duration, calories, average and maximum heart rate) and the weight and body-fat percentage you choose to import into your assessment are sent to your NEX account. Sleep length reaches us only if it is the answer showing in your morning check-in when you submit it. The rest are read on your device, shown to you, and not stored by us.
  • Health Connect (Android) — Health Connect is the health store built into Android, where apps such as Samsung Health, Garmin Connect, Fitbit, Polar Flow, Google Fit and Strava save what your devices record. With your permission NEX reads twelve kinds of record from it:
    • workout sessions — activity type, start and end time, and the name your device gave the session
    • heart rate — the individual readings your watch or strap recorded
    • heart-rate variability — the beat-to-beat variation your watch measures, usually while you sleep. Android and iPhone record this using two different statistics (RMSSD and SDNN), so NEX keeps them apart and never compares one against the other. If you move between an iPhone and an Android phone, your variability history starts fresh — the App tells you when that happens.
    • active calories burned
    • total calories burned — used only as a fallback for a workout your device recorded no active calories for
    • steps
    • distance — how far you moved, over the day and during a workout
    • resting heart rate
    • weight
    • body-fat percentage
    • lean body mass
    • sleep sessions — when you fell asleep and woke up, and the sleep stages your device recorded
    We also read the name of the app that wrote each record (for example "Garmin Connect") so the Fitness screen can show you where your data is coming from.
  • Polar Flow (any device) — when you authorize Polar via OAuth, NEX reads through the Polar AccessLink API: workouts (sport, duration, distance, calories, heart rate, training load, running index, heart-rate zones, fuel split), sleep (duration, score, stages, breathing rate), nightly recharge / HRV, and daily activity (steps, calories, active time).

We only read this data; we never write to your health record and we never change or delete anything in it. On Android the App's manifest declares read permissions only, so there is no write access for it to use. Health Connect lets you choose each record type separately, and NEX works with whatever you allow — you can share workouts and heart rate and withhold the rest.

What we use it for. Health data is used only to run features you can see in the App:

  • Fitness Tracker screen — the workouts you sync are listed there with their type, duration, calories and average and maximum heart rate, along with your totals for the last 7, 14 or 30 days and the names of the apps the readings came from.
  • Athlete assessment — you can import your latest weight and body-fat percentage instead of typing them, and they are saved to your training record.
  • Nutrition plans — the weight and body-fat percentage saved to your assessment feed the calorie and macronutrient targets your plan is calculated from (see 1.4). Your synced workouts do not: training load in the plan comes from the sessions you enter yourself in the Nutrition questionnaire.

What leaves your phone. Two things are sent to your NEX account automatically: the workouts you sync (activity type, start and end time, duration, distance, calories, average and maximum heart rate, and the name of the app that recorded them), and the weight and body-fat percentage you choose to import into your assessment. Where the app that recorded a session gave it a name of its own, that name is what we store and show as the activity type, so any title you typed in Garmin, Strava or Polar Flow travels with the workout.

What stays on your phone. Steps, distance, resting heart rate, exercise minutes, active calories, your heart rate through the day and your heart-rate variability are read to fill the Fitness screen — the activity, heart, sleep and recovery panels, and the weekly trends beside them. Lean body mass is shown beside the other measurements when you import them into your assessment. These readings are displayed on your device and are not sent to our servers or stored by us — for lean body mass there is not even a field on our side to store it in.

Sleep is the one exception, and only through you. Last night's sleep length is read so the morning check-in can offer it as your answer to "hours slept", instead of asking you to type what your watch already measured. It is only a suggestion: it never replaces an answer you have already given, and you can tap a different number. If you then submit the check-in, the number showing in that field — whether it came from your watch or from you — is saved with your check-in, because it is part of the answer you gave. Nothing is sent while you are still filling the form, and if you never submit, nothing is stored. How well you slept is always your own answer; we never take a sleep quality score from a device.

We do not use health or fitness data for advertising, we do not build advertising or marketing profiles from it, and we never sell it or pass it to advertising platforms, data brokers, information resellers, or anyone making credit or lending decisions. NEX is not a medical device and nothing it shows you is a diagnosis or medical advice.

Turning it off. On Android, open the Health Connect app on your phone (on some devices: Settings → Security & privacy → More privacy settings → Health Connect), choose App permissions → NEX and remove any or all of them; Health Connect also lets you delete the underlying records themselves. Apple HealthKit access is revoked on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → NEX, and Polar Flow access from your Polar account or by signing out of the integration in the App. Revoking stops any further reading straight away — it does not remove data already synced to your account, which section 5 explains how to delete.

1.4 AI-generated content

When you generate a personalized nutrition plan, NEX sends a summary of your profile to Anthropic Claude to produce the plan. The summary contains your weight, height, age, sex, body-fat percentage, whether you are a minor, your goal, target weight and weight class, your calculated calorie and macronutrient targets, your training frequency and load, recent recovery and sleep figures, your food allergies (including any you flagged as anaphylaxis risks), intolerances and preferences, any medical conditions, digestive symptoms and medication names you entered, a short note about current pain or injury if you recorded one, your eating habits — including anything you wrote in the free-text "typical day" and food-log fields, up to 600 characters each — and figures derived from your assessment such as waist-to-hip ratio, estimated VO2max and grip asymmetry. Because weight and body-fat percentage can be the values you imported from Health Connect or Apple Health, health-app data can reach Anthropic this way.

The request is sent from our own server function, not from your phone, and we do not send your name, email, photos, phone number, account identifier or any other directly identifying data with it. It is de-identified health data, not anonymous data: it is detailed and sensitive even without your name attached. Anthropic processes the request on our instructions to produce your plan — it is a processing service, not an advertising platform, data broker or information reseller, and we do not authorize it to use your data for anything else. The generated plan is then stored in your account.

1.5 Chat & user-generated content

NEX includes group channels and private messaging between members and coaches. Messages, reactions, and reply quotes you send are stored in our database so other participants can read them. By using these features you agree to our Terms of Service, which prohibits offensive or harmful content. You can block another user by reporting them to a coach/admin via email (kevembusiness@gmail.com), and the academy moderates flagged content within 24 hours.

1.6 Waiver acceptance record

When you accept the academy's liability waiver, NEX generates a PDF containing your name, email, the date and time of acceptance, your IP address at that moment, the document version, and the exact text you agreed to. This PDF is stored in a private bucket in our Supabase storage, accessible only to you and authorized administrators of the academy.

1.7 Camera and Photos

  • The camera is used only to scan the academy's QR code for check-in.
  • You may grant access to your photo library when uploading a profile picture.
  • We never access photos or camera in the background.

  • To calculate your nutrition targets from your body composition and recent training, and to let your coach review your assessments and progress.

  • To communicate with you about the service.

3. How We Share Your Information

Your coach and the academy's staff. NEX is the app of a single physical academy and coaching is what it is for, so any coach or administrator account at that academy — not only the coach you train with — can see your athlete record: your body-composition measurements — including the weight and body-fat entries you import from Health Connect or Apple Health — your recovery and performance logs, your goals and daily check-ins, your attendance, the health profile in which you record injuries, current pain, medications and dietary restrictions, and your nutrition profile with its weight and weekly check-in history. If you do not want a coach to see a measurement, do not record or import it.

The individual workouts you sync from Health Connect or Apple Health are not shown on any coach-facing screen; they appear only on your own Fitness screen.

Sharing a workout yourself. Every workout card on the Fitness screen has a Share button. If you tap it, that single workout's summary (activity type, date, duration, calories and heart rate) is sent to our server, which renders it as an image and hands the image to your phone's share sheet so you can post it wherever you choose. Nothing is shared until you tap Share and pick a destination yourself.

We do not sell your personal data, and we never share health or fitness data with advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers, or use it for personalized advertising or for credit or lending decisions. We share information only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the App:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. (privacy policy)
  • Stripe — payment processing. (privacy policy)
  • Render — backend server hosting. (privacy policy)
  • Apple HealthKit — local-only health-data bridge on iOS; we read data on-device, the OS framework does not transmit anything to Apple's servers on our behalf.
  • Google Health Connect — the on-device health store on Android. We read from it locally on your phone; reading it does not send anything to Google on our behalf. What Health Connect itself holds stays under your control in the Health Connect app.
  • Polar AccessLink — when you connect Polar Flow, we receive your workout, sleep, recovery and activity data through their official API. (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic — used only for AI nutrition plan generation, on our instructions. Receives the de-identified summary described in 1.4, which can include health-app values such as weight and body-fat percentage, and no name, email, photos or account identifier. (privacy policy)
  • Expo / EAS — push notifications and over-the-air updates. (privacy policy)

4. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or accounting purposes (e.g. tax records of payments).

Health and fitness data synced from Health Connect, Apple Health or Polar is kept in your account until you or we delete it, and it is not retained for any legal or accounting purpose — deleting your account removes it with the rest of your record.

5. Your Rights

You may, at any time:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and personal data yourself, in the App, under Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, attendance, training logs, synced workouts, body composition, chat history and signed waiver.
  • Request deletion of individual entries — including workouts synced from Health Connect or Apple Health, and imported weight or body-fat readings — by emailing us.
  • Withdraw health-data access at any time: on Android in the Health Connect app under App permissions → NEX, on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → NEX, and for Polar from your Polar account.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

Deleting data in the Health Connect app removes it from Health Connect; it does not reach copies already synced to your NEX account, and deleting it from your NEX account does not remove it from Health Connect. Use both when you want it gone from both.

To exercise these rights, contact us at kevembusiness@gmail.com.

6. Security

Your data is protected with modern cryptography both in transit and at rest. Everything the App sends to our API and our database — health and fitness data included — travels over HTTPS/TLS and must carry your signed session token, and the databases and file storage of our infrastructure providers (Supabase and Render) encrypt what they hold at rest. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form, and access to your records is restricted by authentication and Row-Level Security policies on our database. Health and fitness data is protected the same way as everything else in your account. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If a minor (13–17) uses the App for training at the academy, registration must be performed by a parent or legal guardian who accepts this Policy on their behalf.
